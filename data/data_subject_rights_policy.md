KENYA AVIATION LIMITED DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS POLICY
6.1	Purpose
Kenya Aviation Limited processes personal data for its employees and customers, as governed by the Data Protection (General) Regulations, 2021. These regulations require the proper handling of data subject requests to ensure compliance and protect the rights of individuals. This document outlines the procedures and responsibilities for effectively managing and responding to data subject requests.
6.2	Objectives
The purpose of this policy is to establish clear guidelines and assign responsibilities for handling data subject requests. It is supported by a detailed Procedure for Handling Data Subject Requests to ensure consistency, accuracy, and compliance with the law.
6.3	Scope
This policy applies to all employees, sub-processors, and third parties involved in processing personal data on behalf of Kenya Aviation Limited. It is mandatory for all internal and external personnel to adhere to the specified rules. The policy remains valid until officially revoked or superseded by a revised version.

6.4	Data Subject Requests
5.4.1	Handling Data Subject Requests

The DATA PROTECTION (GENERAL) REGULATIONS, 2021 defines the requirement for organizations which are processing any type of personal data to enable data subjects to exercise their rights as per ENABLING THE RIGHTS OF A DATA SUBJECT PART II of the DATA PROTECTION (GENERAL) REGULATIONS, 2021.
When Kenya Aviation Limited receives a data subject request, it is necessary that it is evaluated and handled according to the requirements of DATA PROTECTION (GENERAL) REGULATIONS, 2021. For this purpose, a data subject request must be handled in the following way:
1	Request Submission: Data subjects submit their requests using the Kenya Aviation Limited Data Subject Rights Request Form (available at https://bit.ly/Kenya Aviation LimitedDataSubjectRightsRequestForm).
2	Kenya Aviation Limited Evaluates Request: Kenya Aviation Limited staff assess the validity of the request (verifying the data subject and their connection to Kenya Aviation Limited) and confirm the requester's identity. The validity requirements are explained in Section 3.3.
3	Request Type: The request type is identified and processed in accordance with the Procedure for Handling Data Subject Requests (Appendix B).
4	Response: The request is answered following the guidelines provided in Section 3.4 of this document. Timelines for responding to requests must be tracked and adhered to, as detailed in Section 3.5.


5.4.2	Validity of requests
All data subject requests for exercising their rights under the Regulations must be submitted in writing. Oral requests are forwarded to the Data Protection Officer (DPO) for evaluation and determination of validity.
Data subjects can submit requests through any offline or online channel. Kenya Aviation Limited is responsible for ensuring all requests are evaluated and answered carefully, regardless of the channel used.
The data subject must provide enough information to allow Kenya Aviation Limited to verify their identity. If the identity cannot be verified based on the provided information, and Kenya Aviation Limited has reasonable doubts, Kenya Aviation Limited may request additional information for verification purposes.
If a request requires an unreasonable amount of effort or is too complex to fulfill, Kenya Aviation Limited will inform the data subject and will not be obligated to answer the request. In such cases, it's recommended for the data subject to reformulate their request to be more specific and reduce the complexity for Kenya Aviation Limited to respond.

5.4.3	Answering a data subject request
Unless otherwise agreed, the response to the request will be sent via mail to the data subject.
Before closing the request, the following information must be documented on the Kenya Aviation Limited Data Subject Rights Request sheet:

6.4.1.1	Reasons for rejecting data subject requests
A data subject request can be declined under certain circumstances, typically outlined in the THE DATA PROTECTION (GENERAL) REGULATIONS, 2021. Below are the common circumstances under which a request may be declined:
1.	Invalid or Insufficient Identification
2.	The data subject fails to provide sufficient information to verify their identity, raising concerns about potential unauthorized access.
3.	The organization has serious doubts about the identity of the person making the request.
4.	The organization is legally required to retain the data and cannot comply with the request without breaching other laws or regulations.
5.	Granting the request would infringe on the rights and freedoms of others.
6.	The data subject is requesting information or actions that are not covered under applicable data protection laws.
7.	The data subject does not provide sufficient details to allow the organization to process the request, such as missing required forms or information.
8.	The organization does not process or hold the personal data being requested.
9.	The personal data has already been erased in line with the organization’s data retention policy.
10.	The request has been made by someone other than the affected person and, in cases of disability or minority, it has not been demonstrated that the requester is the legal representative.
6.4.1.2	Communication of Decline
In cases where a data subject request is declined, Kenya Aviation Limited shall ensure timely and transparent communication with the data subject. The following steps will be taken:
1.	Notification of Decline
o	The data subject will be promptly notified of the decision within the stipulated response timeframe.
2.	Contents of the Notification
o	The notification will clearly outline:
	Reason(s) for Refusal: A detailed explanation of why the request could not be fulfilled.
	Right to Lodge a Complaint: Information on the data subject's right to escalate the matter to the relevant data protection authority, such as Kenya's Office of the Data Protection Commissioner, for further review or intervention.
5.4.4	Time to answer
A request is considered initially received as soon as a representative of Kenya Aviation Limited takes note of it, or as soon as it reaches the inbox of dataprotectionoffice@Kenya Aviation Limited.com. This is regardless of the validity of the request. A request is considered answered as soon as the initial request is fulfilled (either implemented or rejected). Further inquiries by the data subject trigger a new answer deadline.

5.4.5	General responsibilities

DPO Responsibilities:
The Data Protection Officer (DPO) is responsible for:
1)	Regularly monitoring the Data Subject Request Register for new requests.
2)	Providing an initial response to all data subject requests within 14 days.
3)	Ensuring all requests are resolved within the timelines stipulated in this policy (generally within 7 days or 30 days, with potential extensions as outlined).
4)	Assigning tasks to relevant team members to assist in handling data subject requests.
5)	Informing data subjects of any delays in processing requests, providing clear reasons and revised timelines.
6)	Assisting data subjects in refining their requests if necessary to ensure timely and accurate responses.
7)	Ensuring compliance with all applicable data protection regulations.
Overall Responsibility:
The DPO is ultimately responsible for the proper handling and resolution of all data subject requests received by Kenya Aviation Limited.
