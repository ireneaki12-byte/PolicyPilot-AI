KENYA AVIATION LIMITED DATA RETENTION POLICY
5.1	Purpose

This Data Retention Policy establishes guidelines for the retention and disposal of records and data within Kenya Aviation Limited, in accordance with the Data Protection Act, 2019, and other applicable regulations. This policy ensures information is retained for appropriate durations to meet legal, regulatory, operational, and business requirements, while safeguarding sensitive information and optimizing storage resources.
This Policy also serves the purpose of aiding employees of the company in understanding their obligations in retaining electronic documents - including e-mail, Web files, text files, sound and movie files, PDF documents, as well as all Microsoft Office or other formatted files.
5.2	Scope
This policy applies to all records and data created, received, or maintained by Kenya Aviation Limited, regardless of format (electronic files, paper documents, emails, backups, databases, etc.). This includes, but is not limited to:
a.	Passenger data (e.g., booking information, PNRs, travel documents)
b.	Employee data (e.g., personnel records, payroll information)
c.	Operational data (e.g., flight schedules, maintenance records)
d.	Financial data (e.g., accounting records, transaction logs)
e.	Marketing data (e.g., customer profiles, campaign data)
This policy applies to all Kenya Aviation Limited employees, contractors, third-party processors, and any other entities processing personal data on behalf of Kenya Aviation Limited. All such parties are required to adhere to this policy.
This policy remains in effect until revoked or superseded by a revised version.
5.3	Data Retention and Disposal
4.3.1	Responsibilities
1.	Data Owners (Department Heads/Designated Representatives): Responsible for implementing this policy within their respective departments, ensuring compliance  with local laws and regulations that affect record retention and authorizing data disposal.
2.	Data Protection Officer (DPO): Oversees 
a.	the circulation and yearly review/update and implementation of this policy
b.	provides guidance on data protection matters.
c.	ensures that Kenya Aviation Limited’s employees receive appropriate training and comply with the Policy in terms of personal data handling, data retention and deletion principles.
d.	serves as Kenya Aviation Limited’s point of contact for personal data related issues, and is responsible for notifying the Data Protection Commissioner if a personal data related incident occurs.
e.	is notified of data disposal activities.
3.	All Employees: Responsible for understanding and adhering to this policy and associated procedures.
4.	Quality Department: Responsible for monitoring of this policy.
4.3.2	Data Retention Periods
We may retain personal data for as long as it is necessary to fulfil the purpose for which
it was collected, or as required or permitted by applicable laws. 
We will cease to retain personal data, or remove the means by which the data can be associated with the data subject, as soon as it is reasonable to assume that such retention no longer serves the purpose for which the personal data was collected, and is no longer necessary for legal or business purposes
Data retention periods are determined based on the following criteria:
1.	Legal and Regulatory Requirements: Data must be retained for the periods mandated by applicable laws and regulations (e.g., passenger name records (PNR) retention as per relevant aviation regulations, financial records as per tax laws). Specific retention periods for different data categories are detailed in the Data Retention Schedule (Appendix A).
2.	Operational Requirements: Data necessary for day-to-day business operations (e.g., flight scheduling, customer service) will be retained for the duration required for those operations.
3.	Business Requirements: Data used for business analysis, reporting, or other business purposes will be retained for the period necessary to fulfill those purposes.
4.	Data Minimization: Kenya Aviation Limited strives to collect and retain only the data that is necessary for the specified purposes.
4.3.3	Data Disposal Procedures
1.	Regular Reviews: Data Owners must conduct regular reviews (at least annually) to identify data that has reached the end of its retention period.
2.	Disposal Authorization: Data disposal must be authorized via completion of the Data and Records Disposal Form (Appendix B) and approval from the relevant Data Owner.. The DPO must be notified prior to disposal.
3.	Secure Disposal Methods: Data containing sensitive or confidential information must be disposed of securely using appropriate methods: 
a.	Physical Records: Shredding, secure incineration, or professional data destruction services.
b.	Electronic Records: Secure deletion, data wiping, physical destruction of storage media or and ensuring that backup tapes are destroyed/overwritten.
4.	Data Breach Considerations: In the event of a data breach or suspected data breach, data retention and disposal procedures may be temporarily suspended as directed by the DPO or legal counsel.
4.3.4	Documentation and Audit
•	Retention and Disposal Records: All data retention and disposal actions must be documented, including: 
o	Data category
o	Retention period
o	Disposal date
o	Authorization for disposal
o	Disposal method
•	Audits: The DPO will conduct periodic audits to ensure compliance with this policy. Non-compliance will be reported to senior management, and appropriate corrective action will be taken.
5.4	Data Retention Principles
Kenya Aviation Limited is committed to maintaining the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of all stored data and records. We adhere to the following principles:
1.	Data Security: All stored data and records are protected by appropriate security controls commensurate with their classification level, ensuring confidentiality, integrity, and availability.
2.	Purpose Limitation and Retention Period: Data and records are retained only for the period necessary to fulfill the original business purpose for which they were collected or created, and in compliance with all applicable regulations.
3.	Data Minimization: Kenya Aviation Limited collects and retains only the minimum amount of data necessary for the specified purpose.
4.	Data Disposal (Destruction/Anonymization): Personal data is securely destroyed or anonymized under Kenya Aviation Limited's responsibility and with Data Protection Officer (DPO) approval after the retention period expires.
5.	Extended Retention: While Kenya Aviation Limited is obligated to delete data upon expiration of the predefined retention period, data may be retained for longer periods if required by legal or regulatory obligations. Such extended retention will be justified and documented.
6.	Secure Data Destruction: Kenya Aviation Limited ensures that personal data is destroyed securely to prevent unauthorized access, unlawful processing, or disclosure to third parties.
7.	Third-Party Data Processors: If data destruction is outsourced to a third-party data processor, the engagement will be governed by a written contract that includes a detailed data destruction protocol. This protocol will be maintained by both Kenya Aviation Limited and the data processor.
8.	Technical and Organizational Measures: Kenya Aviation Limited implements appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure the secure collection, retention, and erasure of all data. These measures, along with the methods of data handling, are regularly evaluated and reviewed.
9.	Data Storage and Access: Kenya Aviation Limited maintains sufficient storage capacity and a robust data retention system that facilitates easy data searching, controlled access, and appropriate dissemination.
10.	Employee Awareness: All Kenya Aviation Limited employees and other parties working on behalf of Kenya Aviation Limited receive training and are made fully aware of their individual responsibilities and Kenya Aviation Limited’s obligations under the Data Protection Act, 2019, and this Data Retention Policy.
5.5	Policy Applicability
This policy applies to all physical and electronic records generated by Kenya Aviation Limited's operations, including original documents and reproductions. While this policy outlines Kenya Aviation Limited's internal data handling practices, it does not apply to third parties, who are responsible for maintaining their own data retention policies and complying with applicable regulations. 
5.6	Review 
The Data Retention Policy will be reviewed annually to ensure it remains up-to-date with current legal requirements and best practices. In addition to the annual review, the policy will be reviewed in the following circumstances:
•	Legal Changes: When there are updates to relevant data protection laws or regulations (e.g., Kenya’s Data Protection Act or international regulations).
•	Operational Changes: When significant changes occur in how the organization processes personal data (e.g., the introduction of new technology or procedures).
•	Post-Incident Review: Following any significant data breach or incident, the policy will be reviewed to identify potential improvements.
The Data Protection Officer (DPO), in collaboration with relevant stakeholders, is responsible for reviewing and updating this policy.
